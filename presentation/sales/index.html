The problem

Your feed moves faster than you do — and the posts you actually care about get buried in it.

Overload

🌊 Too much noise

Hundreds of accounts post every day. The one post from the account that matters — an announcement, a new release, an important photo — scrolls away in minutes and is gone.

Missed signals

⏰ You find out too late

By the time you manually check, the moment has passed — the post that needed a reply, a share, or just a look is already days old.

Lost history

🗑️ Nothing is kept

Instagram doesn't archive your feed for you. Posts disappear from view, comments vanish, and there's no way to search what you saw weeks ago.

Checking manually doesn't scale. Nobody can scroll three times a day, remember what they've already seen, and still catch the 1 post in 100 that matters. That's a job for a machine — a tireless one.

The solution

One small system that does the boring work forever: watch, sort, alert, act, archive.

👀 Watch→ 🎯 Classify→ 🔔 Alert→ ⚡ Act→ 🗄️ Archive

👀 It watches for you

Every 5 minutes, the system browses the feed exactly like a real user — quietly and carefully. New posts are detected, remembered, and never processed twice.

🎯 It knows what you care about

You define interest groups — by account, keyword or hashtag. “Everything from these 15 accounts”, “anything about this topic”. The system sorts every post against your groups automatically.

🔔 It pings you where you live

Matched posts arrive in your Telegram — photo, caption and link — each topic neatly separated, seconds after the post appears.

🗄️ It keeps everything

Every post, image and comment is stored in a private archive with a searchable dashboard — your own searchable history of the feed.

🕐 In plain words

  • Every 5 minutes the system wakes up and logs into Instagram as you.
  • It scrolls the feed like a person would, and picks out only what's new since the last visit.
  • Each new post is enriched — full caption, image, comments — then stored and judged.
  • Matched posts are sent to Telegram with the photo and a link; everything is logged.

🛡️ Built to behave

  • Works in small batches (max 10 posts per run) — gentle, low-profile operation.
  • Respects rate limits: when Instagram says slow down, it waits and resumes later.
  • Never repeats: every post is marked seen, so nothing is processed or alerted twice.
  • If something fails, it retries — and tells you only if it truly can't go on.

Step 2 — Classify 🎯

You decide what matters. The system decides what matches — in seconds, automatically.

How matching works

🧩 Three simple rules

  • By account — “any post from these 15 accounts” (e.g. key agencies and organizations).
  • By keyword — “anything mentioning this topic or phrase”.
  • By hashtag — “everything tagged #this”.
  • Posts are re-checked once the full caption is available — a keyword deep in the text still counts.
  • Each match records why it matched, visible in the archive.
